{{<speaker_talk title="How to stop your AI agent from amplifying your team's tech debt">}}
AI only amplifies what your team already does. Without a clear process, the only thing you accelerate isn't delivery — it's tech debt. In this talk, I'll walk through the real workflow we apply on our clients' projects to bring AI into frontend development without losing quality: a new way to run tests inside the very browser the developer already has open, harness engineering as a working framework, and four non-negotiable rules to keep the agent under control.

What makes it different? It comes from months of learning on real frontend projects with clients, where we went from zero AI usage to a fully controlled workflow in production.
{{</speaker_talk>}}

{{<speaker_bio title="Kevin Martínez" tagline="Software Architect at Orbitant" image="img/speakers/kevin.jpeg">}}
Software Architect and maintainer of TWD (testing while developing), a testing methodology designed to rethink how interfaces are validated during the development process. He has a deep passion for sharing knowledge, testing, and accessibility.
